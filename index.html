How to fish

Cast your line into the cove and settle in. Watch for ripples or gentle nibbles around your bobber. When a fish bites, the bobber dips, you'll hear a chime, and the button flashes warm gold.

Tap Reel it in (or press SPACE) to bring your new friend aboard! You can also tap the weather badge anytime to cycle between Morning, Sunset, and Starlight.
