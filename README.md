## Alien Island

A small, self-contained pixel-art survival and terraforming game. No build step or external runtime dependencies are required; open `index.html` in a browser or serve this folder locally:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 3000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:3000`.

### Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys** or the on-screen joystick to move.
- **E / Interact** to gather nearby resources or use a structure.
- Select **Seed** and press **Q**, tap **Use**, or tap nearby ground to plant.
- **Craft** to build a seed beacon or water purifier once you have the materials.
- **Zoom** cycles the camera scale. Progress is autosaved in the browser.

Gather three crystal shards to unlock the seed beacon recipe. Planting seeds and building terraforming structures increase island vitality, bring back vegetation, and improve your oxygen supply.
